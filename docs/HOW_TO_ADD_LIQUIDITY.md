# How to Add Liquidity

This guide explains how a user becomes a liquidity provider in the PODL/LQD ecosystem.

## What Liquidity Means

Liquidity is the LQD or pool capital that helps the network support swaps, routing, and Proof of Dynamic Liquidity reward distribution. A liquidity provider locks funds for a chosen period and receives liquidity power plus rewards.

## Before You Start

- You need an LQD wallet address.
- You need enough LQD balance for the liquidity amount and transaction fee.
- You should understand that locked liquidity may not be instantly withdrawable.

## Add Liquidity From Explorer Wallet

1. Open the LQD Explorer.
2. Go to `Wallet`.
3. Unlock or create your wallet.
4. Open the `Liquidity` tab.
5. Enter the amount of LQD to stake.
6. Choose lock period in days.
7. Click `Stake Liquidity`.
8. Wait for confirmation.
9. Open `Liquidity` or `LP Tracker` to verify your address appears.

## Add Liquidity From Swap DEX

1. Open the Swap DEX.
2. Connect your LQD wallet.
3. Open the `Pool` tab.
4. Select the pair.
5. Approve tokens if required.
6. Enter both token amounts.
7. Review reserves, share, slippage, and price impact.
8. Submit `Add Liquidity`.
9. Confirm the transaction.

## What You Should See

- Your LP address in the LP tracker.
- Your stake amount.
- Liquidity power.
- Total rewards.
- Pending rewards.
- Estimated APR/APY in the liquidity dashboard.

## Remove or Unstake Liquidity

1. Open the wallet liquidity dashboard.
2. Click `Start Unstake`.
3. The chain marks the provider as unstaking.
4. Unlocking happens gradually based on chain rules.
5. Released balance is credited back to the wallet over time.

## Important Notes

- Higher stake and longer lock can increase liquidity power.
- Rewards depend on network activity and reward distribution.
- If manual claim is not active on the node, rewards are auto-synced by reward settlement or unstake flows.
- Always verify the contract/pool before adding liquidity.
